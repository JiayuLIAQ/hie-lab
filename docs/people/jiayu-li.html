About Jiayu

Jiayu Li leads the Healthy and Intelligent Environments (HiE) Lab at NUS. His research connects experimental and computational fluid dynamics with indoor air quality, human exposure and low-energy cooling. He studies how air, heat and contaminants move through the spaces people use, from aircraft cabins and kitchens to tropical buildings and semi-outdoor community spaces.

His work combines laboratory experiments, particle image velocimetry, field measurements, computational fluid dynamics and physics-assisted AI. These methods support the development and evaluation of ventilation, cooling and pollution-control strategies for healthier and more resilient built environments.

Before joining NUS in June 2026, Jiayu held research appointments at UC Berkeley’s Center for the Built Environment, Singapore’s National Environment Agency and the Berkeley Education Alliance for Research in Singapore (BEARS).

Research interests

  • Multiscale airflow, heat transfer and pollution dynamics
  • Human exposure and pollution control
  • Alternative cooling and resilience to extreme heat, haze and pandemics
  • Trustworthy environmental sensing and predictive control
